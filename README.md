## 📱 Overview

SkinScan is a comprehensive telemedicine platform that leverages artificial intelligence to detect skin diseases and facilitate seamless doctor-patient interactions. Upload a photo, get instant AI analysis, and connect with certified dermatologists—all in one app.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍⚕️ For Patients

✔️ **AI-Powered Skin Scanning** – Upload or capture images for instant disease detection  
✔️ **Intelligent Validation** – Automatically rejects non-skin images (objects, walls, clothes)  
✔️ **Detailed Care Guides** – Access symptoms, causes, prevention tips & home remedies  
✔️ **Doctor Discovery** – Browse dermatologists with ratings, experience & specializations  
✔️ **Smart Booking** – Schedule appointments with available time slots  
✔️ **QR-Coded Confirmations** – Receive digital appointment slips with QR codes  
✔️ **Secure Messaging** – Chat with doctors (limited → unlimited after approval)  
✔️ **Digital Prescriptions** – Get auto-formatted prescriptions with hospital details  
✔️ **Appointment Management** – Track all bookings in one place  

### 👩‍⚕️ For Doctors

✔️ **Request Dashboard** – Manage incoming appointment requests efficiently  
✔️ **Approval Workflow** – Accept, reject, confirm or mark appointments complete  
✔️ **Availability Control** – Set working hours, time slots & consultation fees  
✔️ **Online Prescriptions** – Write and send digital prescriptions instantly  
✔️ **Patient Insights** – View patient history, scan results & feedback  
✔️ **Spam Protection** – Block problematic users to maintain quality service  

---

## 🧠 AI Models Used

| Model | Purpose | Detects |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **Skin Disease Classifier** | Primary diagnosis engine | 🔹 Melanoma<br>🔹 Eczema<br>🔹 Acne<br>🔹 Rosacea |
| **Image Validator** | Input verification | Non-skin images (objects, walls, furniture, clothes) |
| **Probability Engine** | Confidence scoring | Disease detection accuracy (0-100%) |

> **Note:** All AI predictions include confidence scores and should be verified by medical professionals.
